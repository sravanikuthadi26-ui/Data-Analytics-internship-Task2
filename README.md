# Task 2 – Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 📌 Objective

Create visualizations that convey a compelling story using charts, graphs, KPIs, and business insights.

## 🛠️ Tool

Tableau / Power BI

## 📊 Dataset

Dataset used: Supermart Grocery Sales Dataset

- Total Records: 9,994
- Columns: 11
- Data Period: 2015–2018
- Dataset was cleaned and date values were properly formatted.

## 📈 Visualizations Created

1. Monthly Sales and Profit Trend
2. Sales and Profit by Category
3. Sales and Profit by Region
4. Top 10 Sub-Categories by Sales
5. Discount vs Profit
6. Overall Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Key Business Insights

- Total Sales: ₹14,956,982
- Total Profit: ₹3,747,121
- Overall Profit Margin: 25.05%
- Highest Sales Category: Eggs, Meat & Fish
- Highest Sales Region: West
- Highest Sales Sub-Category: Health Drinks

## 📖 Data Story

The dashboard shows overall sales and profit performance across different periods, categories, regions, and sub-categories.

The monthly trend helps identify changes in sales and profit over time.

Category and regional charts help compare business performance and identify stronger-performing areas.

The sub-category analysis highlights products with higher sales.

The Discount vs Profit visualization helps understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
